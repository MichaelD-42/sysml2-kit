Seat-switch platform
====================

Example platform project for sysml2-kit: a seat adjustment switch product line (mechanics,
electronics, firmware, with cybersecurity, production and workshop needs) captured as an RFLP
model. Platform requirements (Markdown) play the role customer requirements play in an RFQ
project; an RFQ project imports this model as its ``platform/``.
The compliance page stays empty here: a platform has no customer requirements to answer.
