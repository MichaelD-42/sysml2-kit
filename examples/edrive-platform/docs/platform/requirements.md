# Platform requirements

What the e-drive product line offers every vehicle program. In this project they take the place
of customer requirements: use cases realize them, system requirements satisfy them.

:::{prq} Two traction power ratings
:id: EDU-PRQ-001

The product line shall offer the drive with 50 kW and with 80 kW continuous traction power.
:::

:::{prq} Regenerative braking
:id: EDU-PRQ-002

The drive shall recover braking energy into the vehicle's high-voltage battery.
:::

:::{prq} Torque requests from the vehicle
:id: EDU-PRQ-003

The drive shall take its torque requests from the vehicle control unit over CAN.
:::

:::{prq} One mounting for all variants
:id: EDU-PRQ-004

Every power variant shall fit the same vehicle mounting points.
:::
