# Customer requirements

:::{crs} Daily commute
:id: CRS-001
:class: functional

The vehicle shall take a driver and up to four passengers on a 50 km commute and back
without recharging.
:::

:::{crs} Trailer transport
:id: CRS-002
:class: constraint

The vehicle shall be transportable on a standard car trailer with a 2000 kg payload.
:::

:::{crs} Acceleration
:id: CRS-003
:class: non-functional

The vehicle shall accelerate from 0 to 100 km/h in less than 8 s.
:::

:::{crs} Warranty
:id: CRS-004
:class: non-functional
:scope: commercial

The supplier shall warrant the vehicle for five years or 150 000 km, whichever comes first.
:::
