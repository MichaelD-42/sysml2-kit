Window-switch platform
======================

Example platform project for sysml2-kit: a door window-lifter switch product line (mechanics,
electronics, firmware) captured as an RFLP model. Platform requirements (Markdown) play the role
customer requirements play in an RFQ project; an RFQ project imports this model as its ``platform/``.
The compliance page stays empty here: a platform has no customer requirements to answer.
