# Compliance statements

The supplier's answers that the model does not give: what the platform does not offer, commercial
terms, and deviations from a customer test requirement.

:::{cst} Capacitive comfort keys not offered
:id: CST-001
:answers: CRS-207
:compliance: not-comply

Not offered. The seat-switch platform has mechanical comfort keys with a tactile click; capacitive
keys with active haptic feedback would need a new keypad, sensor electronics and an actuator. A
quotation can be made separately on request.
:::

:::{cst} Warranty terms
:id: CST-002
:answers: CRS-901
:compliance: comply

The supplier warrants the switches for 5 years or 150 000 km from vehicle registration, whichever
comes first, under its standard warranty conditions.
:::

:::{cst} Durability met in part
:id: CST-004
:answers: CRS-304
:compliance: partial

Partial. The platform key mechanism is rated for 200 000 actuations per key and control direction
(CON-104), two thirds of the 300 000 requested. Reaching 300 000 needs a new key mechanism outside the
platform, which can be quoted separately on request.
:::

:::{cst} Durability test pass criterion
:id: CST-003
:answers: CTR-002
:compliance: partial

Deviation. The test is run as CTR-002 prescribes, to 300 000 cycles, but the pass criterion is
200 000 cycles: the platform key is rated for 200 000 actuations (CRS-304 is met in part). The cycle
count at which the first key fails is reported.
:::
