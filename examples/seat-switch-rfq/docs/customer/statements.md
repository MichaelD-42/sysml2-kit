# Compliance statements

The supplier's answers to customer requirements the model does not answer: what the platform does
not offer, commercial terms, and what only a document or a drawing can show.

:::{cst} Capacitive comfort keys not offered
:id: CST-001
:answers: CRS-207
:compliance: not-comply

Not offered. The seat-switch platform has mechanical comfort keys with a tactile click; capacitive
keys with active haptic feedback would need a new keypad, sensor electronics and an actuator. A
quotation can be made separately on request.
:::

:::{cst} Warranty terms
:id: CST-002
:answers: CRS-901
:compliance: comply

The supplier warrants the switches for 5 years or 150 000 km from vehicle registration, whichever
comes first, under its standard warranty conditions.
:::

The requirements below are met and stated as system requirements, but nothing in the model can show
it (a standard's limits, a drawing, a document, a service procedure); these statements answer them.

:::{cst} EMC per OEM-EMC-200
:id: CST-003
:answers: CRS-505
:compliance: comply

Comply. The platform switch has passed emission and immunity tests to severity class 3 of the
supplier's qualification plan QP-SSW-01, which covers OEM-EMC-200; this vehicle's variants are
tested again per CTR-004.
:::

:::{cst} Installation space
:id: CST-004
:answers: CRS-507
:compliance: comply

Comply. The platform housing and knob fit the installation space of the seat trim drawing received
with the request; the supplier's packaging study is attached to the quotation.
:::

:::{cst} Material declaration
:id: CST-005
:answers: CRS-508
:compliance: comply

Comply. All platform materials are free of OEM-MAT-500 restricted substances and will be declared in
the OEM material data system before the first sample delivery.
:::

:::{cst} Debug interface closed in series
:id: CST-006
:answers: CRS-604
:compliance: comply

Comply. The microcontroller's debug port is permanently locked at end of line; series parts have no
programming pads other than the LIN pin.
:::

:::{cst} Replacement from the fitted seat
:id: CST-007
:answers: CRS-804
:compliance: comply

Comply. The switch clips into the side trim and is released with a standard trim tool from the
fitted seat; the connector is reached through the trim opening.
:::

:::{cst} Spare-part numbers per variant
:id: CST-008
:answers: CRS-805
:compliance: comply

Comply. Each combination of housing side and button set ordered for this vehicle line has its own
part number, used as its spare-part number.
:::
